# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, evidence-oriented, and respectful.
- Critique claims, contracts, and trade-offs rather than people.
- Clearly distinguish evidence from inference.
- Accept correction when an attribution or technical claim is unsupported.
- Protect private information and report security concerns privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate misattribution, or publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, harmful, or persistently disruptive.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security reporting channel. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and avoid conflicts of interest.
